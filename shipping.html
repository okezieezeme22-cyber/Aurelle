Shipping information

A little more about how your AURÉLLE order gets to you.

Delivery fees

  • Enugu State: ₦2,000, with complimentary delivery on orders of ₦50,000 or more.
  • South-East states: ₦3,000.
  • Other listed states: ₦4,500.

These are the current demonstration checkout rates. Your delivery fee is shown at checkout before you place an order.

What to expect

Where do you deliver?

The demo checkout supports delivery addresses in the Nigerian states listed at checkout. Confirm that your full address and phone number are correct before placing an order.

How long will delivery take?

Delivery timing depends on your location and is confirmed with your order. This portfolio demo does not connect to a courier or provide live delivery estimates.

How can I track my order?

Sign in with the account used to place the order, then open My account → Orders to see its current status and order history.

Go to My orders
Can I change my delivery address?

Contact us as soon as possible with your order reference. Changes may not be possible after fulfilment begins.
