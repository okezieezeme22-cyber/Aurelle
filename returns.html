Customer care

Returns & exchanges

Need help with an order? Start with your order details so we can assist.

How to request help

  1. Sign in and open My account → Orders to find your order reference.
  2. Contact customer care with the order reference, the item, and whether you need a return or exchange.
  3. Wait for return instructions before sending an item back. Keep the item and its original packaging until we reply.
Ask about a return

Before you contact us

What information should I include?

Include your order reference, the product name, the size and colour, and a short description of the issue. If the item arrived damaged or incorrect, describe what happened.

Can I exchange a size?

Ask us about availability before returning the item. Exchanges depend on stock and must be confirmed by customer care.

When will a refund be issued?

Refund eligibility and timing must be confirmed by customer care after reviewing the order. This demonstration does not process or issue real refunds.

This portfolio demo does not accept or process real returns. Its return and exchange information is illustrative; confirm a final store policy before launch.
