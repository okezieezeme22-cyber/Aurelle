Privacy notice

Last updated: 6 November 2026

AURÉLLE is a portfolio demonstration, not a live shop. This page describes the demo and is not legal advice or a production privacy policy.

Information in this demonstration

The website may save demonstration account details, cart contents, preferences, sample orders and reviews in your browser’s local storage. This lets the demo show its shopping and account interfaces on the same device. The demo has no production customer database or customer support system.

Payments

This static demo does not complete real payments. Do not enter real payment credentials or sensitive personal information.

Third-party services

Web fonts and product photography may be loaded from third-party providers, which may receive standard connection information such as your IP address when your browser requests those assets.

Your choices

You can clear this site’s locally stored demo data through your browser’s site-data or storage settings. Clearing it may remove demo carts, accounts and orders saved on this device.

Contact

For questions about this portfolio demo, use the contact page. Its listed contact details are placeholders and must be replaced before a real store launch.
